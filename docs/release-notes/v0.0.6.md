# Dead Circuit v0.0.6 — Weapon Variety

## Included

- Service Pistol, Relay SMG and Scatterline Shotgun weapon archetypes
- 1/2/3 weapon switching with independent ammo, reload and cooldown state
- data-driven semiautomatic, automatic and multi-pellet hitscan behavior
- HUD weapon name/slot, ammunition and derived build statistics
- shared source-aware damage and all five existing items across every weapon
- clean loadout persistence through rooms and reset on Replay

## Known Limitations

- Weapon models, recoil, animation, audio and balance are prototype quality.
- The shotgun uses a fixed horizontal ray pattern rather than production spread.
- Weapon pickups, upgrades, alternate fire and per-shell reloads are not included.
- Multiplayer networking remains deferred.
